Academy — Free University / College Website Template (uiCookies)

Concept
Academy dresses a research university in a "Heritage Quad" identity — the look of brass plaques,
library wood and stone quadrangles rather than the generic rounded SaaS gradient. The layout is
deliberately editorial and gridded: a two-column hero framed by brass corner brackets, section
eyebrows numbered like chapters (01, 02, 03...), fine brass hairlines used as engraved rules, a
seal-crest logo, and a stately serif display. It is built distinct from its category siblings — it
is a university, not the friendly green e-learning marketplace and not a single school.

Palette ("Heritage Quad")
  #16243D  collegiate navy     (headings, header words, footer, dark bands)
  #7C2B34  heritage garnet     (primary CTA, links, seal book, apply band)
  #B08D4F  antique brass       (hairlines, seal ring, corner frames, numerals)
  #D7B775  light brass         (brass accents on dark backgrounds)
  #ECEDE7  limestone stone     (page background — a hued neutral, not grey or cream)
  #FFFFFF  card white

Font pairing
  Display: Fraunces (400-600, optical sizing) — a characterful old-style serif with gravitas
  Body:    Libre Franklin (400-700) — an institutional American gothic in the Franklin lineage

Tech
  Bootstrap 5 (self-hosted, relative paths) + one custom CSS file + one vanilla-JS file. No jQuery,
  no CDN scripts, no icon fonts — every icon and the seal crest are inline SVG. Interactions
  (mobile nav, compact-on-scroll header, smooth anchor scroll, count-up stats, scroll reveal,
  gallery captions and a validating footer form) are hand-written and guard-claused. Respects
  prefers-reduced-motion and includes a skip link, landmarks, focus-visible styles and
  CollegeOrUniversity JSON-LD. Free for personal and commercial use.
