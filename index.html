02 Why Academy

A small-college feel with a research university behind it.

We keep seminars deliberately small and our library deliberately open. The result is a place where you are known by name and still stand within reach of world-class research the moment you are ready for it.

  • The 24-hour Halloran Library holds 2.1 million volumes and never dims during term.
  • Seminar classes are capped at 18 students, taught by faculty rather than assistants.
  • Sixty research centers share $480M in annual funding — and hire undergraduates.
  • Housing is guaranteed for all four undergraduate years, steps from the quad.

05 Admissions

Your path from applicant to Academy student.

One application, read by real people. We assess the whole person — transcripts, one essay, and what you care enough to build outside the classroom.

  1. 01

    Explore & connect

    Browse programs, join an info session, and speak with the admissions counselor for your region.

  2. 02

    Submit your application

    One form, a personal essay and transcripts. The Common Application is accepted in full.

  3. 03

    Interview or portfolio

    Shortlisted applicants meet a faculty member or submit a portfolio for creative programs.

  4. 04

    Receive your decision

    Regular decisions are released by April 1, each with a full financial-aid package included.

Early Action closes November 1 · Regular Decision closes January 5
